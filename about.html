About Us

Your Trusted Partner for Premium Organic Exports

Committed to Quality, Purity, and Sustainability

Chavan International is a dynamic and fast-growing export company from India, specializing in 100% organic products. Our expertise lies in sourcing and supplying organic jaggery, organic sugar, and other premium organic products, ensuring they meet international standards.

Building Relationships Through Trust and Excellence

We believe in building long-term relationships based on trust and excellence. Our focus is on promoting sustainable farming practices and supporting farmers to deliver natural, chemical-free products, making us a trusted partner for businesses worldwide.
